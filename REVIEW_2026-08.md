## Headline verdict

The project's epistemic infrastructure — anchors, status propagation, PAPER_DIFF, referee notes, the lineage doc, fail-closed tests — is the best I have seen in a working research repo, including published replication packages. The self-correction record (P1 novelty retracted, A5 downgraded to "반증 우세", A3 localized) is exactly what referees say they want and never get.

The problem is not rigor. The problem is that **the repo is now three products running on one engine, and the most defensible product is not the one the name promises.** "Carbon-transition Asset **Pricing**" promises a premium. The premium (bps) is the least identified object in the system — the repo itself says so in every artifact (`SCENARIO_CONDITIONAL`, assumed λ·k·EV). What *is* defensible is a transition-cost **risk decomposition and contract-design engine**. Your complexity anxiety is the correct instinct: the complexity is being spent defending the weakest claim.

## Step 2 — Theory audit

`theory/` is short (489 lines), internally consistent, and unusually honest. Three structural observations:

**(a) The causal chain is right, the emphasis is wrong.** 00_axioms puts the chain as: assets → τ* → T_required → condition gap → anatomy → conditional charge. The strongest empirical statement the model currently produces sits in the *middle* of that chain and is treated as a side finding: **scrap/NG routes are privately NPV-negative over the horizon (JFE/Kobe τ* = None, p_exercise 13–14%); only required pathways and policy packages produce transition** (PAPER_DIFF 갱신 3, s06 finding: H₂ CfD alone does not move τ*; only packages move τ* and gap together; carbon reform closes the gap while *raising* the priced charge). That is a publishable result on its own, it is robust to λ (it lives in the level/timing layer, not the pricing layer), and it speaks directly to the policy audience. It is currently buried under the anatomy.

**(b) A2 is carried honestly but under-priced in the narrative.** The entire sign of the exposure flips if the budget doesn't bind. This is stated as a single-failure-point axiom — good — but the tool layers (underwriting, deal screening) present outputs whose sign is conditional on A2 without re-surfacing it. A badge is not the same as a sign warning.

**(c) The wedge concept is genuinely two theories' intersection** and 10_level_wedge's LEVEL/WEDGE split (Marshallian loss vs option value of waiting, with the σ_project correction replacing the σ_carbon-only attribution) is clean economics. Keeping it quarantined as a closed-form check lane with a separate basis_id is the right call.

## Step 3 — Model correctness

Checked: GBM simulation with Cholesky correlation and per-driver drift; exercise value as growth-annuity-consistent PV (drift-consistency is regression-tested); LSM backward induction with log-polynomial basis; τ* = E[min(τ,H)] with the selection-bias rationale documented; jump mixture σ² = σ²_diff + Σp(ℓ−ℓ̄)²/ℓ̄² with a correctly normalized conditional-on-binding variant paired with E[ℓ|bind] so p_bind is multiplied exactly once; Euler shares w(ρw)/σ² summing to one by identity; asset-level exposure aggregation before firm-level shares (no pre-averaged switch years).

No arithmetic errors found. Two structural approximations remain honestly flagged and are acceptable at this stage: jumps enter the variance layer only (no regime-switching in the LSM), and jump risk carries the diffusion correlation structure (Merton separation OPEN). The RC_k < 0 concern (PAPER_DIFF D4) now has tests (`test_shares_stay_in_unit_interval`, `test_negative_covariance_actually_breaks_the_composition`) — the composition claim is guarded by the nonnegative-correlation bands, and the tests document that this is what guards it. Correct and honest.

## Step 5 — The central logical problem: the anatomy is degenerate for half the sample

Current `shares_by_firm.json`:

| Firm | Route | Dominant driver | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| POSCO | h2_dri | h2 | 73.2% (carbon 21.8%) |
| NIPPON | h2_dri | h2 | 81.4% |
| JFE | scrap_eaf | carbon | **97.7%** |
| KOBE | ng_dri | carbon | **100.0%** |
| KR_NCC / JP_NCC | crackers | feedstock | 96–97% |

A decomposition that returns 98–100% for one driver is not an anatomy; it is a single-exposure statement. The composition claim ("carbon C%, hydrogen H%, electricity E%…" — the sentence the whole project exists to produce, per 00_axioms) currently has empirical content for **two firms out of six**. Worse, the grid-route numbers have drifted from the paper's 79–83% to 96–100% without adjudication (PAPER_DIFF logs the 95.7% state; the current outputs are further still). The two-cluster "finding" — already conceded in A4 as "half result, half route design" — is at 98/2 essentially *all* design: h2=0 is hardwired for scrap routes, and the modeled electricity exposure is too small to matter. The allocator narrative in 06 ("grid-route = short the grid transition") is no longer supported by the model's own output; grid-route firms are, in the model, pure carbon-policy exposure.

This also interacts with PAPER_DIFF's open author item 3 (scope of grid electricity exposure: EAF power only vs full electrification). That definitional choice swings the headline result more than any σ in the calibration. **It is the single highest-priority modeling decision in the project**, ahead of any data fix.

## Step 6 — Identification: where the real freedom is

The repo's defensive energy is concentrated on P1/λ (scalar invariance), which is the *smallest* degree of freedom. Ordered by how much they actually move the shares:

1. **Driver partition (R7, OPEN, unaddressed).** With ρ(h2,elec)=0.7, Euler contributions depend on the coordinate system. No alternative decomposition has been run. For a paper whose headline object is the decomposition, this is the first referee attack, and it is currently met with a literature citation correction rather than an experiment.
2. **Exposure-window and exposure-scope definitions** (PAPER_DIFF author items 1 and 3). Documented as the cause of the 44%→52% POSCO gap and the grid-route inflation.
3. **λ_k non-uniformity (R1).** Your own literature review: 0 supporting, 7 adverse, including sign reversals (Ready 2018) under which "share" ceases to be a composition at all. The repo's stated intent — promote λ_k to a main result — has not structurally happened; `lambda_k_sensitivity.json` shows max shifts of 6.7pp (POSCO) but is still filed as robustness. Note the small shifts for JFE/Kobe are an artifact of degeneracy (a 98% share has nowhere to move), so the current sensitivity understates the problem exactly where the anatomy is weakest.
4. **Scalar λ·p_bind** — identically zero effect on shares (P1). True, tested, and the least interesting fact in the list.

The honest summary sentence for the paper is: *shares are invariant to the pricing scale, conditional on the exposure model, and the exposure model is where the action is.* The theory docs already say this (03_proposition1 is admirably restrained). The product layers do not.

The Nippon case (JP WACC 4% inflating the post-transition H₂ window PV to an 81% share) deserves particular suspicion — a share that is mostly a discount-rate artifact will not survive review.

## Step 8 — The premium layer

π = k·λ·p_bind·σ_B with λ, k, EV all `assumed` means the bps level is a scaling of σ_B by free parameters. The repo knows this and says so everywhere. The economics point the repo does not fully draw: **within the current design, the bps number can never graduate.** No amount of data collection upgrades λ from assumed — that requires the SDF/asset-pricing identification explicitly filed as OPEN and realistically out of scope. So the tool layers (s08–s11) are built around a number that is permanently conditional, wrapped in increasingly elaborate disclaimers (result contracts, evidence grades, basis separation) whose sophistication is itself a signal that the number shouldn't be the headline.

What survives λ: **differences and rankings.** Δπ from interventions (scalar cancels in ordering), the timing-vs-risk instrument classification, the Pareto set, break-even prices, DSCR/NPV gates (which don't touch λ at all). The deal-screening layer's *economics* outputs are fine; its *risk-charge* outputs should be quoted only as Δ and rank.

## Step 9 — Audience fit

For an investor, the genuinely novel and actionable content is: (i) carbon-policy repricing is a sector common factor that stock selection cannot diversify — only direct carbon instruments hedge it; (ii) H₂/electricity/feedstock exposure is selectable via route; (iii) which single contract to negotiate first, and why packages beat single instruments. None of this requires the bps level. For a treasury/CFO, the value is the intervention comparator and the finding that carbon reform is a timing instrument, not a risk-reduction instrument. For policy (your institutional context), the condition gap in MtCO₂ and the package result are the currency. All three audiences are served by the parts of CAP that are identified; none requires the part that is not.

## Step 10 — Recommendations, sequenced

1. **Freeze scope now.** No new s-modules, no new governance layers. Declare s08–s11 + web a "tool track" that consumes frozen artifacts and cannot drive theory-layer changes.
2. **Decide the exposure model before touching data.** Resolve PAPER_DIFF author items 1 (window) and 3 (grid exposure scope) as explicit, anchored decisions. These dominate every calibration issue.
3. **Confront the degeneracy.** Either (a) the grid-route anatomy is reported as what it is — a near-pure carbon-policy exposure, with the two-cluster chart reframed as "composition vs concentration" — or (b) the electricity exposure definition is widened with justification and the anatomy recomputed. Do not ship a 100% share as an "anatomy."
4. **Run the R7 experiment.** One alternative driver partition (e.g., merge h2+elec into "energy"; separately, split carbon_kr policy vs CBAM), rerun s04, report share movement next to the λ_k table. Cheap, and it converts the biggest open referee attack into a robustness section.
5. **Re-anchor the paper on the identified results:** the private-optimum finding (τ*=None for scrap/NG; only packages move τ* and gap; reform raises the priced charge while closing the gap), the condition gap, and the H₂-route anatomy with λ_k bands as a main table. The bps level moves to an appendix as an illustration.
6. **Rename the claim, if not the project.** What is defensible is transition-cost risk decomposition and contract identification, not asset pricing. One sentence in the abstract fixes this; the SDF ambition stays as OPEN future work.
7. **Then** fix the data problems you've found — within the frozen exposure model, so fixes are interpretable. Priority per the model's own sensitivity: firm registry (PROVISIONAL entries), GCAM surrogate saturation (38 < 41.3 Mt, which pushes late assets' T_required to the horizon), Nippon WACC treatment, JEPX/SMP series.
